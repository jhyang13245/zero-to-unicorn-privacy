Zero to Unicorn Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026
Developer: TRACECELL

This policy explains how TRACECELL ("the developer") handles information in the Android game "Zero to Unicorn" ("the app").

1. What the developer collects

The app has no sign-up or login, and the developer runs no server for it. The developer does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or photos, and the app does not send such information to the developer.

2. What is stored on your device

Your game progress (saved games) and settings are stored on your device only. They are not sent to the developer or backed up elsewhere by the app. Deleting the app deletes them.

3. Ads and the ad SDK

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show ads and measure how they perform, the Google AdMob SDK may collect the following from your device:

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising ID
  • Your IP address and the approximate region inferred from it
  • Device information such as device model, operating system version and app version
  • Ad usage data such as impressions and clicks, and crash and performance diagnostics

Google collects and processes this data under Google's own privacy policy. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. The developer does not receive or keep this data.

Ads are shown when the device is online. The app has rewarded ads (watch an ad to receive an in-game reward) and interstitial ads before the month-end statement and the management meeting and on the game-over screen.

4. Consent and your choices

  • Where the law requires consent (for example the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for ad-related consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) and lets you change your choice in the app.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off personalized ads in Settings > Google > Ads.

5. Age

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

6. Sharing and international transfer

The developer does not sell or share your personal information with third parties. Through the ad SDK, Google processes the data listed in section 3, and that data may be transferred to Google servers outside your country.

7. Retention and deletion

The developer keeps no data about you. Game data on your device is deleted when you delete the app. How long Google keeps ad SDK data follows Google's policies.

8. Your rights

The app has no accounts, so the developer holds no personal data about you. For questions or requests about privacy, contact the address below.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new text and its effective date will be posted on this page.
