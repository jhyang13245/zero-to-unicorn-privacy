Zero to Unicorn Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026
Developer: TRACECELL

한국어

This policy explains how TRACECELL ("the developer") handles information in the Android game "Zero to Unicorn" ("the app"). It is written for Korea's Personal Information Protection Act and also covers the EU and EEA General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR, US state privacy laws, Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information and Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (sections 12 to 18).

1. What the developer collects

The app has no sign-up or login, and the developer runs no server for it. The developer does not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise (GPS) location, contacts or photos, and the app does not send such information to the developer.

2. What is stored on your device, and backup

Your game progress (saved games) and settings are kept only in the app's storage on your device. They are not sent to the developer. However, if you have Android backup turned on, Android's own backup may copy the saved games to your Google account. That backup is between you and Google; the developer does not receive or see it. Deleting the app deletes the saved games from your device. A backup in your Google account may remain for a while after you delete the app, depending on Android's backup settings; you can delete it from your Google account.

3. Ads and Google's collection of behavioral data

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. The developer allows the Google ad SDK (Google Mobile Ads SDK) built into the app to collect the information below directly from your device. The ad SDK sends it straight to Google LLC. The developer does not receive or collect this information and does not store it on any server.

  • Company that collects and processes the behavioral data: Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA
  • Collection tool: the Google ad SDK. It uses device identifiers (the advertising ID and the App Set ID) and may use cookies or similar technologies in the in-app web view that displays an ad.
  • How it is collected: the ad SDK collects it automatically while the app runs and when ads are requested and shown, and sends it to Google over the internet, encrypted in transit with TLS.
  • Items:
    • Advertising ID and App Set ID (device identifiers) and, where applicable, identifiers associated with the account signed in on the device
    • App usage and ad interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views
    • IP address and the approximate location inferred from it
    • Device, operating system and app information, such as device model, OS version and app version
    • App and SDK performance diagnostics
  • Purposes: showing ads (including personalized ads based on your interests), measuring and analyzing ad performance, and preventing fraud and abuse and keeping the service secure
  • Retention and use period: under Google's policies. https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention
  • How to refuse: delete your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads as described in section 4.
  • Contact and remedies: Google, googlekrsupport@google.com; Google's privacy policy, https://policies.google.com/privacy; how Google uses information from partner apps, https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can also contact the developer at the email address in section 10, or the bodies in section 11.

Google processes this information under its own privacy policy and may process it on servers outside Korea, including in the United States (see section 15). The developer does not sell personal information (see section 17).

4. Automatic collection (cookies, advertising ID) and how to refuse it

The developer does not use cookies in the app. The ad SDK automatically collects device identifiers (the advertising ID and the App Set ID) and may use cookies or similar technologies in the in-app web view that displays an ad. Usage data collected this way (behavioral data) may be used for personalized ads. This policy web page does not set cookies. To refuse:

  • Android 12 or later: Settings > Privacy (or Security & privacy) > Ads > Delete advertising ID
  • Android 11 or earlier: Settings > Google > Ads > turn off ad personalization
  • Menu names may differ by manufacturer and device.
  • Where the law requires consent (for example the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for ad-related consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), and you can change your choice in the app settings.
  • With the device's internet connection off, the ad SDK sends nothing.

If you delete the advertising ID or turn off personalized ads, you can still use the app, but ads may still appear. While the internet connection is off, you cannot use the features that give rewards for watching ads.

5. Where ads appear

  • Rewarded ads (you choose to watch an ad and receive an in-game reward): 20x and 50x speed, instant talent search, asking investors again, emergency funds, doubling a release's publicity, doubling earnings made while you were away, and extra funding after bankruptcy
  • Interstitial ads: they may appear when you close the month-end statement with its button, and after a rewind or ending the company, chosen on the game-over screen, has taken effect. Interstitial ads appear at most once every 5 minutes of real time, and only if one has already been loaded.

6. Retention and deletion

The developer keeps no personal information about you, so there is nothing to delete. Game data on your device is deleted when you delete the app (for the backup in your Google account, see section 2). How long Google keeps the information it collects follows Google's policies, as in section 3.

7. Security

The developer runs no server or database that holds your personal information. Data the ad SDK sends to Google is encrypted in transit with TLS. Saved games are kept in the app's private storage on your device.

8. Your rights and how to use them

You may ask to access, correct, delete or stop the processing of your personal information. You may also exercise these rights through a legal representative or a person you authorize. The app has no accounts, so the developer holds no personal information about you, but you can send a request to the email address below and we will reply within 10 days. For information Google collects, you can use the steps in section 4, your Google account's ad settings and the information at https://policies.google.com/privacy. If you are in the EEA or the UK, see also section 16; if you are in the United States, see also section 17.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children. It is not directed to children under 14 (the age used in Korea's Personal Information Protection Act) or to children under 13 (the age used in the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA), and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from them. In the EEA and the UK, the age at which a person can consent to online services on their own ranges from 13 to 16 depending on the country. The app is intended for users aged 16 and over.

The information in section 3 is collected by the Google ad SDK and sent directly to Google; the developer does not receive it. The developer's part is to include the ad SDK in the app, decide where ads appear (section 5) and show Google's consent message where the law requires it.

13. Legal bases for processing (EEA and UK)

  • Consent: for personalized ads, and for storing or accessing information on your device (such as the advertising ID or cookies) for ads. The app asks for this consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), which uses the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF).
  • Legitimate interests: for showing non-personalized ads so that the app can be offered free, for limiting how often ads are shown (frequency capping), and for preventing fraud and keeping the ads secure. Where any of this requires storing or accessing information on your device, that step relies on your consent unless the law does not require consent for it. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests (section 16).
  • Game data: saved games and settings stay on your device (section 2). The developer does not receive or process them.
  • Email: if you write to the developer, your email address and message are used only to reply to you and to handle your request, based on the developer's legitimate interest in answering and, for requests about your rights, the legal obligation to respond. They are kept only as long as needed for that.

14. Withdrawing or changing consent

You can withdraw or change your consent at any time.

  • In the app: Settings > Ad privacy settings. This button appears where the law requires it, for example in the EEA and the UK, and opens Google's consent message again.
  • On your device: delete the advertising ID or turn off ad personalization as described in section 4.

Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before you withdrew it. After you withdraw it, ads may still appear without personalization.
